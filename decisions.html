Locations that were wrong

All 120 addresses in the app were checked against maps. Three were wrong, and one claim was overstated. Corrected in the app, flagged on the card where it changes the day.

The Gianicolo, 28 Sept — the significant one

Terrazza delle Quadrighe is the rooftop terrace of the Vittoriano at Piazza Venezia — across the river from the Gianicolo, and a place you already visit on the 29th. The sunset stop is now Piazzale Giuseppe Garibaldi, which is the actual Janiculum panorama, uphill from Trastevere. Open terrace, no ticket, no closing time.

Ferramenta, Palermo — moved, not dropped

It is at Piazza Giovanni Meli 8 in the Vucciria, not Piazza Sett'Angeli behind the Cathedral — the opposite side of the centre from Palazzo dei Normanni, so it was unusable as the 12:30 lunch. It has become the 20:30 option instead, where its Saturday hours (12:00 – 01:00) suit. Lunch on the 26th is now an honest "pick somewhere on the Corso". One warning: grilled squid is one of Ferramenta's signature dishes — relevant for Ceci.

Casa Del Vino is not a wine window

Via dell'Ariento 16R is correct, but it is a historic counter-service enoteca among the San Lorenzo stalls, not one of Florence's buchette. It has come off the wine-window trail; Babae on Via Santo Spirito 21R is the real thing. Its Tuesday 15:30 close checks out, so the 12:15 slot is still its only window.

Softened rather than guessed

Caffè Giacosa now reads Via della Spada without a street number, and the Negroni line says the bar claims the drink rather than stating it as fact. Where a number could not be verified, the name and street are enough for Maps and safer than a wrong pin. Stops that are not places — clearing immigration, a buffer, the walk home — deliberately carry no Maps button rather than a guessed pin.

The Singapore consulate — I had this wrong

Singapore has no embassy in Rome, only an Honorary Consulate-General, and the Via Marche 2 address I first listed was invented. Independent directories disagree on its real address — Via Frattina 89 or Via Parma 22 — so check rome.mfa.gov.sg rather than trusting either. The office was closed 3 Aug – 4 Sept 2026, so it is open again before you fly.

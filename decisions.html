2 · Ticketing — booked since, so the pricing questions are closed

Six of the flagged items were about price or availability. All are now booked and paid, which settles them regardless of what the guides quoted.

  • St Mark's Basilica — resolved, and the important one. The doc assumed a free nave with a €3–5 skip-the-line; tickets are in fact online-only with no desk on site. Booked for 11:00, visit 11:00–11:30. The itinerary stop has moved from 10:45 to match.
  • Doge's Palace. Booked; price question closed. Noted in the app: you're using the palace only, not the Correr, Archaeological Museum or Marciana the same ticket covers.
  • Venice Accademia. Booked. The €9-versus-€20 question no longer applies.
  • Duomo dome climb — the split is now in the app. The Brunelleschi Pass is for four. Grandma and Sarah hold the non-climb pass and take the Baptistery, cathedral floor and Opera del Duomo Museum while the others do the 463 steps, then everyone regroups. No large bags, so the cloakroom detour isn't needed.
  • Palatine Chapel. Booked for 1:30pm, chapel and Royal Apartments. This also retires the hours conflict.
  • Trattoria Zà Zà. Confirmed since — moved from pending to confirmed.

The four Palermo gaps stay filled with my suggestions, labelled "My suggestion" in the app and to be played by ear on the day: Ferramenta for the 26 Sept lunch, I Segreti del Chiostro for the cannolo stop, the Vucciria bars for the late night, and the Via Roma / Via Maqueda corridor for Sunday shopping, since much of Palermo closes.
